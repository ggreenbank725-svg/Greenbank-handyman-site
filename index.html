What we handle

Handyman services

Practical repairs and fixes around the house, done right the first time.

General repairs

The loose, broken, sticking, or worn-out things around a house — the jobs that pile up until someone finally takes care of them.

Electrical fixes

Outlets, switches, light fixtures, ceiling fans, and garage or under-cabinet lighting. Straightforward electrical work done with care and respect for how it should be done.

Plumbing repairs

Faucets, shutoff valves, supply lines, toilets, and small leak repairs. Clean, solid work — not a temporary patch you have to redo next month.

Drywall patching

Holes, dents, and cracks in walls or ceilings, patched and blended so the repair doesn’t become the first thing you see.

Painting

Touch-ups, a room, trim, doors, or a small exterior section. Clean lines and a fresh, finished look.

Furniture & TV mounting

TVs, shelves, and furniture mounted securely into the wall. Heavy pieces are handled with a plan, not a shrug.

Door & window repair

Doors that stick or won’t latch, worn hardware, screens, and window issues that make a house drafty or frustrating to live in.

Fence & deck repair

Loose boards, sagging gates, wobbly rails, and weathered sections. Repair work to make what’s already there sound again.

Assembly

Furniture, shelves, gym equipment, storage sheds, and the boxes that come with “some assembly required.” Built square, tight, and ready to use.

Van & RV builds

12V lighting, solar, rooftop AC, headliners, and TV mounts for vans and mobile builds. Electrical and finish work kept at a handyman scale.

Small roof repairs

Patching a damaged section — rotted decking, underlayment, and shingles.

Yard & outdoor

Yard and planter cleanup, irrigation timer installs, and other outdoor jobs that keep a property tidy and working.
